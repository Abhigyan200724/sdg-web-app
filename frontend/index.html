Sustainable Development Goals

Transforming our world through sustainable development by 2030.

About SDGs

The 17 Sustainable Development Goals are a universal call to action to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure peace and prosperity.

Sustainable Development Goal 1 (SDG 1) aims to end poverty in all its forms everywhere by 2030. It goes beyond simple income tracking, targeting both extreme monetary poverty (living on less than US $3.00 per day) and multidimensional poverty by focusing on equal rights, access to basic services, and social protection systems.


The Core Challenges:


  • Extreme Poverty: Despite long-term declines, recent global economic shocks and conflicts have slowed progress, with over 800 million people still surviving in extreme poverty worldwide.


  • Lack of Access to Basic Services: Many people, especially in rural and marginalized communities, lack access to essential services like healthcare, education, and clean water.


  • Weak Social Protection Systems: In many countries, social protection systems are inadequate or inaccessible, leaving vulnerable populations without the support they need to escape poverty.


Key Targets for 2030:


  • Eradicate Extreme Poverty: Completely wipe out extreme poverty for all people globally.


  • Reduce Poverty by Half: Halve the proportion of men, women, and children of all ages living in poverty according to national definitions.


  • Implement Social Protection:Ensure substantial coverage of the poor and vulnerable through nationally appropriate social protection floors.


  • Equal Rights & Basic Resources: Ensure equal rights to economic resources, basic services, land ownership, and new technologies, particularly for the most vulnerable groups.
